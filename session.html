FAQ

Real answers.

What exactly is the Offer Playbook?

A personalised strategy document built from your answers — covering your action plan, pitch, offer structure, DM scripts, sales call blueprint, objection responses, content ideas, and your next move. Not a template. Built from what you actually told us.

How long does the whole process take?

The intake form takes about 10 minutes. The playbook is ready the same day. We can then go through it on a 30-minute screen-share, or I can send it directly — your choice.

Do I need to be on a call?

No. But most people find the screen-share useful — it's where we walk through each section together and you can ask questions in real time.

Who is this best suited for?

Coaches, consultants, and service-based business owners who know what they do but struggle to position it clearly, price it confidently, and talk about it without sounding scripted or salesy.

What do I need to bring?

Nothing. Just answer the 5 intake questions honestly. The more specific you are, the sharper the playbook.
